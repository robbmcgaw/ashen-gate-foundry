One look for every Ashen Gate window

Every specimen on this page is drawn with the real shared stylesheet and General Manager's real skin files. Parts marked “New in kit 2” come from the draft additions that would ship to all modules in one release.

Pick a skin at the top and the whole page follows. Tap a skin card to do the same.

Elevation and motion

Open decisions

Robb will be asked these one at a time. The suggested answer is under each question.

  1. D1Keep today’s look (gold on charcoal plus the seven skins) as the base?Yes. The audit found drift, not a bad direction.
  2. D2New … buttons gold (primary) or plain?Plain with a +. Gold stays for Save/Apply, one per view.
  3. D3Character Hub keeps its icon rail?Yes, it is a sheet. Labels become tooltips.
  4. D4Player Access saves immediately or with Save changes?With Save changes, like other settings.
  5. D5Which rarity colours?Hub’s hues, Common turned grey, one set in the kit.
  6. D6Ledger Rare/Exotic finds look like item rarity?No, their own badges.
  7. D7Narrow tabs: scroll or wrap?Scroll sideways.
  8. D8Help as a dialog or a page in the window?Dialog with Close, everywhere.
  9. D9Show Alpha/Beta in headers?No. Stage lives in the CHANGELOG.
  10. D10Renown folder list beside every tab?Yes on Group, Player, Organizations; Pantheon full width.
  11. D11Foundry tooltips instead of native hover text?Yes, module by module.
